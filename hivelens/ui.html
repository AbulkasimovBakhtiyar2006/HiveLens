      How it works

      Models look at the photos, plain code does the measuring, and the report can only say what the measurements support.

      1. 1sam-3.1

        Find

        The board photo is cut into 600 px tiles, and unclear dark spots are checked again at 300 px. On frames, SAM outlines the comb and every bee.

      2. 2muse-spark-1.3

        Review

        Spots SAM does not recognise go onto numbered sheets of 36, and Muse Spark marks the mites. Frame tiles are labelled honey, brood, larvae, pollen or empty.

      3. 3opencv, numpy

        Measure

        Every candidate must look like a mite: the right size, reddish-brown, a smooth oval. Rules then set the status from the numbers.

      4. 4tool calls

        Report

        Muse Spark states what it expects before it sees the numbers. Each claim is checked, and findings without a verified claim are dropped.

      Limits

      Comb reading has no benchmark yet. We found no openly licensed frame photos with expert labels, so frame results were checked by eye.

      The default thresholds in hivelens/config.py are 3 mites a day to watch and 10 to act. Adjust them for your season and colony size.

      Demo hives are marked as demo data everywhere, so they never mix with real measurements.
